Bank logos used as small identification icons (64 px, on white), bundled so the app works offline.

- SBIN, HDFC, PUNB, BARB, AIRP: Wikimedia Commons (SBI-logo.svg; HDFC Bank Logo.svg; Punjab National Bank new logo.svg;
  Bank of Baroda Logo since Dec 19.png; Airtel Payments Bank logo 2.svg), symbol cropped from the wordmark.
- ICIC: icicibank.com apple-touch-icon. PYTM: paytmbank.com favicon.
- AXIS, KKBK: logo files from the team's EQUINEXT project (public/logos).
- IPOS: India Post emblem (Wikimedia Commons India-post-logo.jpg); India Post Payments Bank is part of India Post.

The marks belong to the banks; they are used only to identify each bank in the officer's view.
